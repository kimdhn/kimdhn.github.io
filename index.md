I’m Dohoon Kim, a first-year M.S.-Ph.D. student at the <a href="https://gsds.snu.ac.kr/">Graduate School of Data Science</a>, Seoul National University, working at the <a href='https://www.skiml.snu.ac.kr/'>SKIML Lab</a> under the supervision of <a href="https://leejayyoon.github.io/index.html">Prof. Jay-Yoon Lee</a>. <br>

I received my Bachelor's degree in <a href="https://hyds.hanyang.ac.kr/">Data Science</a> and <a href="https://bme.hanyang.ac.kr/">Biomedical Engineering</a> from Hanyang University, Seoul. During my undergraduate studies, it was a great honor to be advised by <a href="https://dsybaik-hy.github.io/">Prof. Sungyong Baik</a>.
